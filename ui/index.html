01 — Programmatic prompt optimization

Can optimized prompts beat hand-written ones? Not on this task.

We replaced the hand-written prompt of a bank-support intent classifier with prompts generated by DSPy, TextGrad and AdalFlow, then measured the difference on 12 intents from Banking77.
